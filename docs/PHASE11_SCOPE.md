# Phase 11 Scope

Phase 11 delivers the lifestyle/daily-life simulation foundation: home quality and crowding, furnishings/upgrades, vehicle reliability and service, contextual style/wardrobe state, food strategy/cooking/family meals, outings, travel, hobbies, pets, household services, devices, schedule conflicts and lifestyle milestones.

This phase intentionally keeps room placement and fully visual outfit/travel presentation out of scope; those belong to the later visual-experience phase.
