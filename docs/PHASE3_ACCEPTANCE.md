# Phase 3 Acceptance Criteria — Relationships & Households

Phase 3 is complete when:

1. Relationship feelings remain directional and hidden, while canonical partnership status is shared truth.
2. Attraction eligibility respects age, sex and romantic orientation without exposing compatibility math to the player.
3. Dating, exclusivity, cohabitation, engagement, marriage, breakup and divorce can occur without guaranteed acceptance.
4. NPC-to-NPC romance can form without player involvement.
5. A household is a canonical object separate from a partnership and may persist/change independently.
6. Moving in closes superseded households rather than leaving duplicate active homes.
7. Household schedules, labor burden and finance style are represented explicitly.
8. Player free-text conversation works offline through deterministic intent/tone parsing.
9. AI is not required to decide or validate relationship outcomes.
10. Family planning is negotiated state. Planned biological pregnancy only occurs when circumstances support it.
11. Pregnancy is canonical and births create a new Tier 1 person with inherited traits, human state and development state.
12. Biological, adoptive, step, spouse and sibling links can persist independently of household membership.
13. Parenting changes development gradually rather than granting guaranteed outcomes.
14. Separation of a shared household creates distinct homes and a custody/co-parenting record for shared children.
15. Shared custody can move a child between households on the calendar.
16. Existing v1/v2 saves migrate to v3 without losing prior memories, secrets, ledger history, relationships or events.
17. Same seed + same player actions + same elapsed time remains deterministic.
