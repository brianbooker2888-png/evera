# Phase 11 Acceptance — Lifestyle, Daily Life, Possessions & Travel

EVERA v0.11 is acceptable only when the following remain true:

- Existing v9 saves hydrate to schema v10 without losing earlier systems.
- New worlds start with canonical lifestyle, home, wardrobe, device and hobby state.
- Lifestyle simulation remains deterministic and fully offline.
- Food strategy changes household economics and health/time tradeoffs without duplicating finance ownership.
- Home comfort, maintenance, organization, privacy and space pressure respond to household state.
- Home upgrades spend canonical checking cash and persist as possessions.
- Owned vehicles gain lifestyle reliability/maintenance state and can break down deterministically.
- Wardrobe/style are contextual identity state, not RPG armor.
- Hobbies build skill/recreation and can carry ongoing cost.
- Pets are persistent household members with cost, health, attachment, aging and mortality.
- Travel reserves calendar time, spends real money, persists destination/companions and creates timeline milestones.
- Household services trade money for time rather than generating free benefits.
- Technology devices wear out and can be replaced.
- Schedule conflicts can create missed commitments.
- Lifestyle milestones persist into the broader life history.
- The release passes production dependency security, strict simulation typecheck, the cumulative Vitest suite, production build and temporary iOS/Android wrapper generation.

Deferred to Phase 12/14 depth:
- Real destination databases, visa systems and location-specific attraction catalogs.
- Detailed room-by-room placement.
- Fully visual wardrobes/outfits and cinematic travel scenes.
- Native 3D home/furnishing interaction.
